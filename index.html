Questions

Frequently asked

Is CarpoolKaro really free?
Yes. Sign-up is free. You only pay ₹2 platform fee per booking. Pay the actual fare directly to the driver via UPI. No hidden charges, ever.
How do you verify users?
Every user must verify with phone OTP and work email from a recognized IT company. We also check company badges. Optional ID upload for premium trust.
What if I need to cancel my booking?
The ₹2 platform fee is non-refundable if you cancel. If the driver cancels, you get your ₹2 back. This keeps everyone accountable and fair.
How does the ₹10 referral work?
Share your referral code. When your friend joins and completes their first ride, both of you get ₹10 in your wallet. No limit on referrals.
Which cities do you cover?
Live in 6 cities: Hyderabad, Bangalore, Pune, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Chennai. Each city has its own ride pool with local IT hubs and metros pre-loaded.
Can I post recurring rides?
Absolutely. Post once and select "Daily" — we'll auto-create rides for every weekday on your route. Perfect for fixed office commutes.
How do payments to drivers work?
Drivers receive the full fare directly through UPI. The app shows a payment picker (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, BHIM, etc.) so you can use any UPI app.
