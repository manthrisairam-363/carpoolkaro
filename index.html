Questions

Frequently asked

Is CarpoolKaro really free?
Signing up, browsing, posting and requesting cost nothing. When a car owner approves your request, ₹2 is charged to your wallet and ₹2 to the owner's. The fare itself goes to the owner directly — the app never touches it.
What is the fare, and who sets it?
The car owner sets a per-seat contribution towards fuel and tolls for their regular commute. You see it before you request. It is a cost share between colleagues going the same way, not a taxi fare.
How do you verify users?
Everyone signs in with Google. You can also add your work email — we confirm it with a one-time code and show your company on your profile. Each ride shows the owner's photo, car model and number plate, and both sides can rate every trip.
What if I cancel, or the owner never replies?
Nothing is charged until the owner approves. A request that isn't answered before the ride expires on its own. If you cancel after approval the ₹2 isn't refunded, but you can request another ride within 2 hours at no extra fee. If the owner cancels the ride, your ₹2 comes back.
How does the ₹10 referral work?
Share your referral code from the app. When a colleague joins with it, you both get ₹10 wallet credit. Each new user can apply one referral code.
Which cities do you cover?
Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Pune, Mumbai, Delhi NCR and Chennai. Within a city, rides are matched by locality and zone, so you see the ones on your route first.
Can I post a daily ride once?
Yes. Save your commute — home, office, times, days — and the app posts your ride every working day. Pause it when you're on leave or working from home.
How do I pay the owner?
After the ride, the app shows the owner's UPI ID or mobile number and a QR code. Pay from any UPI app — PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm or your bank's app — then tap "I've paid". The owner confirms it on their side.
